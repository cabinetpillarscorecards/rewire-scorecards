OKR Guide — WFUSM Academic Cabinet

What are Aspirational OKRs?

Aspirational OKRs set ambitious, stretch targets that drive transformational performance. They are NOT performance reviews.

Scoring Principle

  • 70% achievement = excellent (grant-funding analogy: hitting 70% of stated aims = funded, high-performing grant)
  • 100% = target was not ambitious enough
  • Below 40% = recalibration needed

Status Indicators

  • 🟢 On Track — progressing as planned
  • 🟡 At Risk — needs attention or course correction
  • 🔴 Off Track — escalation or replanning required

Confidence %

Team confidence of achieving this Key Result by the due date. Update quarterly or when conditions change.

Lock for Cabinet

Freezes all editing. Use before presenting to Steering Teams or Dean Boulware.
