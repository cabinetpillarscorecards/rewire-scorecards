Key Principles (Google OKR Playbook)

• Consistently scoring 1.0 = goals not ambitious enough — raise the bar

• Committed OKRs: Must achieve 100% — realistic stretch targets

• Aspirational OKRs: Moonshots — 60–70% = success

• KRs must describe outcomes, not activities

• OKRs ≠ KPIs — KPIs monitor health; OKRs drive change
